----------------------------

[CUSTOMER/ORGANIZER/ADMIN]
        |
        v
(1.0 AUTHENTICATION & ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT) <----> ||USERS||
        |
        +---- role, account state and authenticated session

[ORGANIZER] --> (2.0 MANAGE EVENTS & TICKET TYPES)
                       |----> ||EVENTS||
                       |----> ||TICKET_TYPES||
                       |----> ||EVENT_STATUS_HISTORY||
                       +----> ||NOTIFICATIONS||

[ADMIN] --> (3.0 REVIEW ORGANIZERS & EVENTS)
                       |<---> ||USERS||
                       |<---> ||HALLS & EVENT_CATEGORIES||
                       |<---> ||EVENTS||
                       |----> ||EVENT_STATUS_HISTORY||
                       +----> ||NOTIFICATIONS||

[CUSTOMER] --> (4.0 BROWSE APPROVED EVENTS)
                       |<---- ||EVENTS||
                       |<---- ||EVENT_CATEGORIES||
                       |<---- ||TICKET_TYPES||
                       +----> event details, prices and live availability

[CUSTOMER] --> (5.0 RESERVE INVENTORY & CREATE PAYMENT ORDER)
                       |<---> ||EVENTS & TICKET_TYPES||
                       |----> ||BOOKINGS & BOOKING_ITEMS||
                       |----> ||PAYMENTS||
                       |----> [RAZORPAY ORDERS API]
                       +----> checkout order details

[CUSTOMER] --> [RAZORPAY STANDARD CHECKOUT]
                       |
                       +----> payment identifiers and signature
                       v
(6.0 VERIFY PAYMENT & CONFIRM BOOKING)
                       |<---- [RAZORPAY / SIGNED WEBHOOKS]
                       |<---> ||PAYMENTS||
                       |<---> ||PAYMENT_WEBHOOK_EVENTS||
                       |<---> ||BOOKINGS||
                       |<---> ||TICKET_TYPES||
                       +----> ||NOTIFICATIONS||

(7.0 EXPIRE FAILED/ABANDONED RESERVATIONS)
                       |<---> ||BOOKINGS||
                       |<---> ||PAYMENTS||
                       +----> ||TICKET_TYPES|| (release reserved quantity)

[CUSTOMER] --> (8.0 CANCEL BOOKING & REQUEST REFUND)
                       |<---> ||BOOKINGS||
                       |<---> ||TICKET_TYPES||
                       |----> ||REFUNDS||
                       |<---> [RAZORPAY REFUND API]
                       +----> ||NOTIFICATIONS||

[ORGANIZER] --> (9.0 REQUEST EVENT CANCELLATION)
                       |----> ||EVENT_CANCELLATION_REQUESTS||
                       +----> ||NOTIFICATIONS||

[ADMIN] --> (10.0 PROCESS EVENT CANCELLATION & REFUNDS)
                       |<---> ||EVENT_CANCELLATION_REQUESTS||
                       |<---> ||EVENTS||
                       |<---> ||BOOKINGS||
                       |<---> ||TICKET_TYPES||
                       |----> ||REFUNDS||
                       |<---> [RAZORPAY REFUND API]
                       |----> ||EVENT_STATUS_HISTORY||
                       +----> ||NOTIFICATIONS||

(11.0 DASHBOARDS & NOTIFICATIONS) reads users, events, bookings, ticket types,
payments, refunds, cancellation requests and notifications, then provides
role-appropriate status and reports.


DFD LEVEL 2 - PAID BOOKING FLOW
-------------------------------

[CUSTOMER SELECTS TICKETS]
        |
        v
(VALIDATE EVENT, SALES WINDOW AND TOTAL QUANTITY <= 10)
        |
        v
(BEGIN DATABASE TRANSACTION)
        |
        v
(LOCK EVENT, THEN TICKET TYPES IN ASCENDING ID ORDER)
        |
        v
(RECHECK AVAILABLE = CAPACITY - RESERVED - SOLD)
        |
        +-- unavailable --> ROLLBACK --> "Tickets no longer available"
        |
        v
(CALCULATE PRICES ON SERVER)
        |
        v
(CREATE PENDING_PAYMENT BOOKING + ITEMS)
        |
        v
(INCREASE RESERVED_QUANTITY; SET 10-MINUTE EXPIRY; COMMIT)
        |
        v
(CREATE RAZORPAY ORDER ON SERVER AND OPEN CHECKOUT)
        |
        +-- failed/abandoned/expired --> release reservation transactionally
        |
        v
(VERIFY CHECKOUT SIGNATURE AND CAPTURED PAYMENT SERVER-SIDE)
        |
        v
(TRANSACTION: LOCK EVENT, BOOKING AND TICKET TYPES)
        |
        v
(MOVE RESERVED_QUANTITY TO SOLD_QUANTITY)
        |
        v
(MARK PAYMENT CAPTURED + BOOKING CONFIRMED + COMMIT)
        |
        v
[BOOKING CONFIRMATION]

Webhook processing is idempotent. Duplicate webhook delivery must not confirm a
booking or move inventory more than once.


DFD LEVEL 2 - CANCELLATION AND REFUND
--------------------------------------

[VALID CANCELLATION]
        |
        v
(TRANSACTION: LOCK EVENT, BOOKING AND TICKET TYPES)
        |
        v
(CHANGE BOOKING TO CUSTOMER_CANCELLED OR EVENT_CANCELLED)
        |
        v
(DECREASE SOLD_QUANTITY; CREATE PENDING REFUND RECORD; COMMIT)
        |
        v
(CALL RAZORPAY REFUND API WITHOUT HOLDING DATABASE LOCKS)
        |
        +-- accepted --> store Razorpay refund ID and track processing
        +-- failed ----> store failure and permit safe retry
        |
        v
(SIGNED REFUND WEBHOOK / API VERIFICATION)
        |
        v
(UPDATE REFUND STATUS AND NOTIFY CUSTOMER)
