EVENT BOOKING SYSTEM - DATABASE TABLE GUIDE
===========================================

This system uses 13 main database tables. Each table has one clear purpose.

1. users
Stores customer, organizer, and admin accounts, including login details, role,
account status, and organizer approval information.

2. halls
Stores the fixed hall's name, address, contact details, and maximum capacity.

3. event_categories
Stores admin-managed categories such as Music, Comedy, Theatre, and Workshop.

4. events
Stores event information, organizer, category, hall, schedule, sale period,
capacity, approval status, poster path, and cancellation details.

5. event_status_history
Keeps a short history of event approvals, rejections, resubmissions, and
cancellations, including who performed each action and why.

6. ticket_types
Stores an event's ticket options, such as Standard, Premium, and VIP, along with
their price, capacity, reserved quantity, sold quantity, and active status.

7. bookings
Stores the main customer booking record, public booking reference, event,
booking status, total quantity, total amount, and reservation expiry time.

8. booking_items
Stores the individual ticket types inside a booking. It preserves the quantity
and price paid so old bookings remain correct after ticket prices change.

9. payments
Stores Razorpay order and payment references, amount, currency, payment status,
signature verification time, and payment success or failure details.

10. refunds
Stores refund requests and their Razorpay status, amount, reason, responsible
user, processing time, and any failure information.

11. payment_webhook_events
Records Razorpay webhook events so repeated notifications are handled safely and
the same payment or refund is not processed more than once.

12. event_cancellation_requests
Stores an organizer's request to cancel an approved event and the admin's final
approval or rejection decision.

13. notifications
Stores dashboard notifications for customers, organizers, and admins, including
booking confirmations, approval results, cancellations, and refund updates.

IMPORTANT SUMMARY
-----------------
- users manages people and access.
- halls, event_categories, events, and ticket_types manage events and capacity.
- bookings and booking_items manage customer ticket orders and price history.
- payments, refunds, and payment_webhook_events manage Razorpay activity.
- event_status_history and event_cancellation_requests preserve accountability.
- notifications keeps every user informed through their dashboard.

No sensitive card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or Razorpay secret key is stored in these
tables. Events, bookings, payments, and other important history are preserved
instead of being physically deleted.
